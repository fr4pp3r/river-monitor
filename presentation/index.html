02 · Problem & setting

Why Barangay Elvita needs a local warning

Flood context
  • Flood-prone barangay on the Narra, Palawan coast
  • Advisories arrive municipality-wide — they do not say whether this barangay is affected
  • Connectivity fails exactly when warnings matter most
Design constraint: works without internet — classification, storage, and dashboard run on a local Raspberry Pi 5

Setting

Barangay Elvita

Narra, Palawan · river-monitoring prototype for the BDRRMC · barangay-scale, offline-first early warning

03 · Objectives & scope

Objectives & scope

Decision-support framing

In scope

  • Offline-first pipeline: sensor → LoRa → Pi 5 → dashboard + SMS
  • Risk classification: Random Forest + 20-rule fuzzy logic
  • Dual-horizon forecasts: 24 h and 48 h side by side
  • Field deployment and acceptability check

Out of scope

  • Validated flood-prediction skill
  • Measured warning lead time
  • Field-tested SMS delivery
  • Community-wide generalization (n = 2 survey)
Framing: a feasibility study — the system advises; the BDRRMC decides.

06 · Data pipeline

Data pipeline — one minute at a time

Ingestion formulas & forecast cache

Every 60 seconds — LoRa

  • water level = 2.5 m − sensor distance
  • rainfall = tip count × 0.2 mm
  • stale-data warning after 5 silent minutes

Every 6 hours — Open-Meteo

  • weather + tide forecast fetched
  • cached locally for 6 hours
  • 7-day outlook in SQLite; classification never waits on live internet
Formulas run on the Pi; only the forecast refresh needs internet.

12 · Limitations

Limitations — stated plainly

What the study does not claim

1. Labels from the same 8 features

94.26% is rule agreement, not prediction skill

2. River never rose above Receding

Alarm / Critical never field-tested; no SMS sent

3. One battery, 21.5 h

Power and sensor reliability need hardening

4. n = 2 respondents

Acceptability is preliminary, not generalizable

Every limitation carries a recommendation in the conclusions.
13 · Contributions & conclusion

Contributions & conclusion

What this thesis adds
  • Offline-first, barangay-scale prototype — about ₱23,700
  • Hybrid RF + fuzzy pipeline on a single Raspberry Pi 5
  • Feasibility established — predictive skill is future work, not a claim
  • Honest evaluation: limitations disclosed, recommendations listed
A decision-support tool, not an autonomous warning system — the BDRRMC makes the call.
